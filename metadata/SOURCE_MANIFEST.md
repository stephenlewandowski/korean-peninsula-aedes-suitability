## Citation and interpretation

The scientific paper is a preprint: Siddiqui et al., “Suitable seasons: Global monthly habitat suitability for the arbovirus vectors *Aedes aegypti* and *Aedes albopictus* in 1975–2024,” DOI `10.64898/2026.04.17.719149`. The dataset is model-derived environmental suitability and is not a disease-risk or confirmed-presence dataset.
